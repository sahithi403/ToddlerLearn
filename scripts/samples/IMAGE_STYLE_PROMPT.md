# Image Generation Style — ToddlerLearn

All generated images for this project must use the following style prompt. Append the subject to the end.

## Base Style Prompt

> 2D cel-shaded children's illustration. Color palette: warm sandy beige (#D4B87A) and muted golden ochre (#E8C45A) as background base tones. Vegetation in desaturated olive (#8A9A4A) and warm straw yellow (#D4B030), rendered as loose flat brushstroke shapes. Ground surfaces in textured sandy tan (#C8A870). Rocks in cool gray (#9AA5A8). Paper grain and sand noise texture overlaid at low opacity across all surfaces — no smooth gradients, no clean digital edges. Characters built from simplified geometric shapes: large circular heads, rounded bodies, minimal anatomy detail. Eyes: large circular pupils (#1A1A2E), colored iris in muted teal or amber. Each character limited to 3 flat fill colors. Primary focal character: white or off-white body (#F0EDE0) with one saturated accent color in orange-red (#E05A1A) or slate blue (#6B9BAF). Dark markings in deep navy (#1E2A3A), not pure black. No outlines or thin strokes — shapes defined by color contrast only. Warm, low-saturation overall with 1–2 high-saturation accent regions. Matte finish, no gloss or specular highlights. Static, calm composition with low visual complexity.

## Usage
Append your subject to the end of the base prompt. Example:
> ...low visual complexity. **A small white rabbit sitting beside a mossy stone.**
